Tutor's Closet
📚 About the Project
Tutor's Closet is a platform designed to connect students with suitable tutors for personalized offline learning.
Unlike traditional online tutoring platforms, Tutor's Closet focuses on helping students find tutors who can provide face-to-face classes at convenient locations and times. The platform acts as a bridge between students/parents looking for academic support and tutors seeking flexible teaching opportunities.

🎯 Problem Statement
Finding a reliable and suitable tutor can be difficult for students and parents. At the same time, many qualified students, professionals, and teachers may be looking for flexible part-time teaching opportunities.
Tutor's Closet aims to solve this problem by creating a platform where:
Students or parents can search for suitable tutors.
Tutors can register and showcase their skills.
Students can select tutors based on their requirements.
Both students and tutors can manage their learning and teaching schedules.

💡 Solution
Tutor's Closet provides a centralized platform with separate portals for Students/Parents and Tutors.
The platform allows users to:
Search for tutors based on subject and requirements.
View tutor profiles and teaching details.
Request or book tutoring sessions.
Manage tutoring schedules.
Provide personalized and flexible learning opportunities.
Help tutors find teaching opportunities.

✨ Features
👨‍🎓 Student/Parent Portal
Student registration and login
Search for tutors
View tutor profiles
Select subjects and learning requirements
Request tutoring sessions
Manage bookings and schedules
View tutor information

👩‍🏫 Tutor Portal
Tutor registration and login
